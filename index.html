Papers

FSE 2027 · Under review

Video Text Understanding Across Diverse Scenes: An Evidence-Guided Benchmark

ICLR 2027 · Under review

Research Assistant · Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology

An evidence-guided benchmark for interpreting on-screen text together with visual and temporal context. The construction workflow combines structured evidence planning, automated verification, text-only filtering, and human correction to evaluate joint text–visual understanding.

Selected projects

GRU-Based Market Forecasting

Kaggle Jane Street Competition · Quantitative modeling

A recurrent forecasting approach that models each trading day as a sequence, combining market-wide averages and per-symbol rolling statistics with auxiliary prediction targets. Two GRU architectures are ensembled across random seeds, while time-based validation and online updates using newly available labels address temporal generalization and changing market conditions.

GRUAuxiliary targetsOnline learningEnsembling

Sequential Advertising Recommendation

Tencent Advertising Algorithm Competition · Recommendation systems

An HSTU-based recommendation approach combining gated attention, rotary position embeddings, relative time bias, and Fourier time encoding. User and item features are fused with multimodal representations, while InfoNCE training uses in-batch negatives and sampling-probability correction to learn retrieval representations.
